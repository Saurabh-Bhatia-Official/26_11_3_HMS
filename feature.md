# CareSync - Features List (Layman's Guide)

Welcome to **CareSync**! This guide explains all the features of the Hospital Management System in simple, non-technical language so that anyone—hospital staff, doctors, administrators, and patients—can easily understand what the software does.

---

## 1. 🏥 Patient Registration & Medical Records
* **Quick Patient Registration**: Easily register new patients by saving their name, contact details, date of birth, gender, and emergency contacts.
* **Unique Patient ID**: Every patient gets a unique hospital ID so their records are never mixed up.
* **Complete Health History**: Store blood group, chronic conditions, and previous illnesses in one place.
* **Allergy Warnings**: Highlight dangerous allergies (such as penicillin or food allergies) in bold red banners so doctors and nurses never miss them.
* **Instant Search**: Find any patient instantly by typing their name, phone number, or patient ID.

---

## 2. 📅 Outpatient Department (OPD) & Appointments
* **Book & Manage Appointments**: Schedule appointments with specific doctors for any date and time slot.
* **Smart Waiting Token Queue**: Generates token numbers for patients waiting to see the doctor to avoid crowding and long arguments.
* **Vitals Check Station**: Nurses or assistants can quickly record blood pressure, pulse, body temperature, oxygen level (SpO2), and weight before the consultation.
* **Doctor Consultation Desk**: Doctors can review patient history, enter symptoms and diagnosis, and write treatment notes.
* **Digital Prescriptions**: Doctors can generate clear, typed prescriptions with medicine dosage and instructions (e.g., "1 tablet after food twice daily").

---

## 3. 🛏️ Inpatient Department (IPD) & Bed Management
* **Visual Bed Board**: Color-coded view of every bed across all hospital wards (General Ward, Semi-Private, Private Room, ICU, and Emergency).
* **Live Bed Status**: See at a glance which beds are **Available** (Green), **Occupied** (Red), or **Cleaning/Under Maintenance** (Orange).
* **Easy Patient Admission**: Admit a patient to any available bed in just two clicks.
* **Bed Transfers**: Move a patient from one bed or ward to another (e.g., from Emergency to ICU or General Ward) without losing track of their billing.
* **Smooth Discharge**: Automatically frees up the bed once the patient is discharged and settles their bill.

---

## 4. 💊 Pharmacy & Medicine Inventory
* **Live Stock Tracking**: Real-time counter of every medicine in stock, including tablet strips, syrups, and injections.
* **Batch & Expiry Date Management**: Track different batches and expiration dates so expired medicines are never sold or given to patients.
* **Low Stock & Expiry Alerts**: Automatically flags medicines that are running low or nearing expiration so you can reorder on time.
* **Prescription Dispensing**: Pharmacists can pull up a doctor's prescription directly on their screen and dispense the exact prescribed medicines.
* **Over-the-Counter Sales (POS)**: Sell general medicines and medical supplies to walk-in customers with instant receipt generation.
* **Automatic Stock Deduction**: Selling or dispensing medicine automatically decreases the warehouse stock count.

---

## 5. 🔬 Laboratory & Diagnostic Tests
* **Test Ordering**: Doctors can order lab investigations such as CBC blood tests, urine analysis, lipid profile, X-rays, etc.
* **Step-by-Step Test Tracking**: Track lab orders as they move through each stage:
  * Sample Collected ➔ In Progress ➔ Completed.
* **Result Entry with Normal Reference Ranges**: Lab technicians can enter test results along with normal health ranges so abnormal values stand out.
* **Lab Reports**: Ready-to-print lab test reports for patients and consulting doctors.

---

## 6. 💰 Billing & Invoicing Desk
* **All-in-One Patient Billing**: Automatically combines all hospital charges into a single clean invoice:
  * Doctor consultation fees
  * Bed charges (calculated by number of days)
  * Pharmacy medicine charges
  * Laboratory and scan charges
  * Nursing and procedure fees
* **Multiple Payment Options**: Accept payments through Cash, Credit/Debit Card, UPI / Online Transfer, or Insurance (TPA).
* **Advance & Partial Payments**: Record advance deposits and track remaining unpaid balances.
* **Instant Receipts**: Print or view itemized bills and payment receipts for patients and insurance claims.

---

## 7. 🏢 Multi-Hospital & Branch Support (Super Admin)
* **Manage Multiple Hospitals**: Healthcare chains can manage multiple hospital branches or clinics from a single system.
* **One-Click Hospital Switcher**: Super administrators can switch between different hospital branches in seconds without needing to log out and log back in.
* **Centralized Oversight**: Add new hospital branches, assign addresses, and configure local facility types.

---

## 8. 🔒 Role-Based Security & Permissions
* **Job-Specific Access**: Every hospital employee only sees the screens and data they need to do their job:
  * **Doctor**: Consultations, vitals, medical records, and prescriptions.
  * **Nurse**: Inpatient bed board, vitals entry, and patient care.
  * **Pharmacist**: Medicine inventory, stock inward, and dispensing.
  * **Lab Technician**: Laboratory test queue and result entry.
  * **Receptionist**: Patient registration, appointments, and token queues.
  * **Billing / Accounts**: Invoices, payments, and receipts.
  * **Hospital Admin**: Staff accounts, departments, and general hospital settings.
  * **Super Admin**: Complete control across all hospitals and system configurations.
* **Auto-Lock Screen**: Protects sensitive patient data by locking the application when the computer is left unattended. Quick PIN or password unlock.
* **Audit Trail**: Keeps a security log of important actions (who created a patient, who modified an invoice, etc.).

---

## 9. 📊 Dashboards & Analytics
* **Executive Overview**: Visual summary at the top of the screen showing:
  * Today's total outpatient visits
  * Current occupied vs. available beds
  * Today's revenue and pending payments
  * Pending lab orders and low-stock warnings
* **Filterable Reports**: View daily, weekly, or monthly reports on patient volume, revenue, and departmental activity.

---

## 10. ⚡ Works 100% Offline (No Internet Required)
* **Zero Downtime**: The entire system runs directly on your computer using built-in local database storage.
* **Internet Outage Proof**: If the internet cable is cut or the Wi-Fi goes down, your hospital operations continue without a hitch.
